    How this works — in four lines

    1. Every day we scan Nigerian entertainment blogs and news feeds for mentions of each housemate.

    2. Mentions, comments and shares become a daily engagement score (CPI) per housemate.

    3. A Bayesian model learns each housemate's momentum and eviction risk from those scores and the eviction record.

    4. 10,000 simulations of the rest of the season produce the win probabilities, podium projection and at-risk panel.
